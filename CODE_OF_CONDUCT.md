# Code of Conduct

We want everyone who takes part in Elpino to be able to do so without harassment, whatever their background,
experience or location.

## Our standard

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and receive it graciously.
- Credit others' work, and ask when you are unsure rather than guess.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, slurs, threats or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies to every space used for the project: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any
chat or meetings held for it.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to **conduct@elpino.chat**. Reports are handled privately and taken seriously. Maintainers may
respond with a warning, temporary or permanent removal from the project, or other proportionate action, and will
not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
